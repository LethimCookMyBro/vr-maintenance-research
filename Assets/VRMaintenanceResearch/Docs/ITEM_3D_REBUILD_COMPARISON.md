# Rejected integration vs rebuild

Left column is the rejected commit `b6b5777` (preserved on tag `reject/item3d-b6b5777`).
Right column is the rebuild. All captures are offscreen `Camera.Render()` frames, which
never draw gizmos.

## Computer — open side

| Rejected | Rebuild |
|---|---|
| ![rejected](Screenshots/Rejected_ITEM3D/Rejected_Computer_OpenSide.png) | ![rebuild](Screenshots/Audit/ITEM3D_Final_Computer_OpenSide.png) |

The rejected interior has the cooler standing off against the rear wall, the card tilted
in free space, and the supply below the shell. The rebuild seats the board on its tray,
the cooler on the socket, the memory in the slots the board draws, and the supply in the
basement.
